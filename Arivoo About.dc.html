Our story

Why we built arivoo.

Institutions were juggling a dozen disconnected tools — one for admissions, another for fees, a spreadsheet for attendance and yet another for exams. Every handoff meant re-typing data, chasing approvals and losing time.

We set out to replace that patchwork with one platform where every module shares a single record. Today arivoo brings university management, school management, learning and secure online assessment together — so teams spend their time on students, not software.

What we believe

The principles behind every release.

One connected record

Every module reads and writes the same data, so nothing is entered twice and nothing falls through the cracks.

Built with institutions

Features come from working alongside registrars, principals, teachers, students and parents.

Secure by design

Role-based access, encrypted data and full audit trails come as standard on every plan.

Here for the long run

Onboarding, training and support from a team that stays with you well after go-live.
